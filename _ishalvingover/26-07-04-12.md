### On the platform.

I have returned to this ground!
This rural town, of mediaeval houses,
has been awared its fame from my work
eternal.

Ignorants are my enemies, and those
who support particles of such, they that
rot and decompose, like Apples of Sodom,
I amassed them here, by nature.

How it transports, explodes & implode:
Firstly th' imperfect flows.
Hope within, concentrates nearby
and sails to an isolated island.

Where the night air glimmers --
Partitioned, accepting only by portion
the visitors. The lone yet near land...
